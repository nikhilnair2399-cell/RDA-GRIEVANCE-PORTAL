Quick Reference
How the grievance system works
How submissions arrive: Grievances are submitted via Formspree and delivered to rdaaiimsb@gmail.com. Each email has a reference number in the subject line (e.g. [RDA-2603-19143527]).
Replying to grievants: Simply hit "Reply" on the Formspree notification email — the Reply-To header is automatically set to the grievant's email. Alternatively, use the composer above with pre-formatted templates.
Anonymous submissions: If the grievant chose to remain anonymous, no email or personal info is included. You can only communicate updates through phone (if provided) or via the portal's tracking page.
Urgency levels: 🔴 Immediate — respond within 24 hours. 🟡 High Priority — respond within 3 days. 🟢 Routine — respond within 7–14 days.
Supporting documents: Grievants are instructed to email documents separately to rdaaiimsb@gmail.com with their reference number. Check the inbox for attachments matching the reference.
Escalation protocol: If a grievance involves harassment, discrimination, or requires institutional action, refer to ICC or the relevant authority. Use the "ICC Referral" template to notify the grievant.
